Basic Skill: Light Discharge
Avery discharges light and bends it around him in a myriad of ways, dealing [Light] damage.
Stage 1: Launches a beam of light, dealing damage equal to 6% of health. Applies [Blind] on hit opponents.
Stage 2: Slashes in two arcs in separate directions, dealing 9% of health as damage.
Stage 3: Charges up a ball of light and thrusts it at opponents, dealing 12% of health as damage and inflicting [Burn]. If opponent already has the debuff upon attacking, deal an immediate instance of [Burn] but with self’s crit stats added.
[Blind]
Debuff: Prevent target from attacking for 3 seconds.
[Burn]
Debuff:Deals continuous [Fire] damage equal to 0.5% of max health to self every other second for 5 seconds.
