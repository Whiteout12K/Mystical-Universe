Basic Skill: Meteor Bash
Ted sends heavy kicks of metal-enhanced moves against opponents, dealing [Earth] damage. The damage dealt becomes [Shield] for self equal to 15% of its value.
Stage 1: Thrusts several quick fists at the opponent, dealing 40% of defense as damage.
Stage 2: Launches spheres of metal towards opponents in a line in a short point range, dealing 65% of defense as damage.
Stage 3: Slams a metal wall in front, dealing 80% of defense as empowered damage.
[Shield]
Buff: Creates an overshield that absorbs damage equal to a certain amount of health.
