Technical Skill: Lightning Discharge
Allies and self with lightning based attacks (stated in skill descriptions, but usually without special effects) will have their respective elements converted to [Lightning Element] damage (applies to all skills that deal element damage). Each character whose element is converted will still inherently keep the former’s elements’ buffs, resistances and auras.
[Lightning Element]: A special elemental attack which shreds barriers, reducing hit opponent’s defense by -25% when lightning attack lands. The lighting attack will also apply a [Charged] counter on opponents which stack up to 5 times. This element can maintain its lightning element aura for a short time on inflicted opponents.
[Charged]
Buff: Does the following:
> At five stacks, if having [Lightning Element] aura and next damaged by [Water] attacks, deals small AoE damage equal to 350% of the inflictor's attack to self which is considered [Lightning Element] damage.
> If having [Water] aura and hit by [Lightning Element] attack, a smaller single-hit damage is dealt to self equal to 100% of the inflictor's attack which is considered [Lightning Element] damage.
The damage is increased by +15% for each level of the skill used to deal damage. The damage dealt from this effect can crit, equal to the inflictor’s crit rate and damage.
Damage is further also increased by +1% for each point of piercing of the inflictor.
