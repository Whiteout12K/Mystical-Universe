Basic Skill: Ice Manipulation
Kaden unleashes a valley of ice that encircles and traps opponents, dealing [Water] damage.
Stage 1: Hurls ice chunks at nearby opponents, dealing small AoE damage equal to 100% of attack as damage.
Stage 2: Summons spikes of ice to emerge from the ground around opponents, dealing AoE damage equal to 125% of attack and remaining on-field for a short time. Inflicts the [Freeze] debuff on hit opponents.
Stage 3: Explodes the ice spikes, dealing 165% of attack as AoE empowered damage.
[Freeze]
Debuff: Stops action for 2 seconds.
