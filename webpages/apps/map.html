About this atlas

Geography follows the Mystical Districts glossary in Beginning and The Messengers’ Return, their story chapters, and the author’s Mystical Legends Notes. Island boundaries and individual site positions are illustrative, not surveyed coordinates. District 9 is unnamed in the glossary. Story descriptions may contain spoilers.

Earth coastlines: Natural Earth, via world-atlas 2.0.2. All map assets are hosted on this website.
