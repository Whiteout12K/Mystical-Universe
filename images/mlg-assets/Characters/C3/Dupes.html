For every opponent hit, Ted will gain a stack of [Let'm Cook] which lasts indefinitely. He inflicts [Attack Down I] on nearby opponents every 5 stacks gained.
[Let'm Cook]
Buff: Upon reaching increments of 10 stacks (10, 20, etc…), Ted will gain the [Invention] effect (Infinite Stacks).
[Invention]
Buff: A specific action may occur with this effect (Infinite Stacks). Reduce movement speed by -20% per stack while this effect is active. Any gained [Shield] will give damage reduction of +30% while the effect is active.
[Attack Down I]
Debuff: Reduces attack by -15% (Duration: 10 seconds).
Each stack of [Let'm Cook] will increase defense by +0.25%.
Ted’s Technical Skill effects will also be applied to the party for the same duration.
Gain a [Super Shield] for self equal to 150% of defense after using any special skills, the effect is shared amongst the party. Whenever the dash skill is used, gain a stack of [Let'm Cook] (Cooldown: 2 seconds).
The inflicted debuff [Attack Down I] is enhanced to [Attack Down III] and an additional debuff [Crit Down II] is inflicted.
[Super Shield]
Unremovable Buff: A special shield that requires empowered attacks to break, otherwise all other incoming attacks are reduced by -75%. The shield is represented by a break bar above health and will persist until broken. If the bar is broken, the active character will be frozen for 8 seconds and will take +300% increased incoming damage during the duration.
[Attack Down III]
Debuff: Reduces attack by -45% (Duration: 10 seconds).
[Crit Down II]
Debuff: Reduces crit rate by -20% (Duration: 10 seconds).
Increases the damage of special skills by +15%.
Charge energy gauge by +10% per stack of [Invention] gained. When Ultimate Skill: Metal Mode is used, each stack of [Invention] present before being cleared will increase Ted’s overall damage by an additional +30% per stack for the duration of the ultimate.
Rapidly consuming all dash skills will automatically give Ted the effect [Gamer] and [Tinkering]. These effects are considered effects from a Technical Skill. Whenever either of these effects end, Ted’s special skill cooldowns will reset.
[Gamer]
Buff: Increases defense by +30% for 15 seconds.
[Tinkering]
Buff: Crit rate increases by +25% for 15 seconds.
