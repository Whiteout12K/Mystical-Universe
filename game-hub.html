The Mystical Legends game is a 2D top-down pixel RPG game with an open world to explore and many combat scenerios to face. Journey with the main cast of the Mystical Legends, with Avery, Ted, River, and Kaden, and explore the world with freedom: from the beginning stories to the unseen. Play as a unique role where you are the Main Character to grow stronger and choose your own unique path.
