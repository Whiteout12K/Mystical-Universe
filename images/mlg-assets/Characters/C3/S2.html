Special Skill: Rods of Steel
Launches a large beam of steel towards opponents, dealing [Earth] empowered damage equal to 240% of defense. Hit opponents will gain the effect [Dazed].
If [Invention] is active, several more beams (+3 more of them) will be shot out, dealing the same damage. Clear a stack of [Invention].
[Dazed]
Debuff: Become susceptible to critical hit damage for 2 seconds.
Cooldown: 10 seconds
Mana Cost: 130 MP
