Special Skill: Cubic Trinity
Kaden summons three cubes that swirl around him, lasting a brief time, and will implode upon contact with an opponent, dealing 350% of attack as AoE [Water]empowered damage. The cubes can remain even when Kaden is off-field.
For each cube imploded, he will gain a stack, up to three stacks, of the buff [Ice Prince]. After each cube implodes, they remain for 10 seconds on-field, dealing 180% of attack as AoE [Water] damage per second.
[Ice Prince]
Buff: Gain a percentage of critical hit rate and crit damage increase equal to each point, up to 2% of max defense (Stackable, Duration: 15 seconds).
Cooldown: 15 seconds
Mana Cost: 110 MP
