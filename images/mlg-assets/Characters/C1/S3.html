Special Skill: Light Compression
Condensing light around him into any object, Avery gains newfound versatility. Hold down the skill button to cycle through weapons and letting go will select the specific weapon as the new active basic skill.
Basic Skill: Compressed Light Sword - A denser weapon, increases basic skill damage by +30%, keeping original basic skill stages. Every other hit will be an empowered attack.
Basic Skill: Compressed Barrier - Creates a barrier of light in front, with health equal to 1000% of stat used by the basic skill (Cooldown: 15 seconds).
Basic Skill: Compressed Light Bow - Creates a ranged damaging weapon; +50% more attack when hitting more than 10 meters, but +50% damage taken. Uses preset basic skill Stage 1 damage multiplier per projectile. Hold basic skill to charge up a powerful 3-arrow attack.
Basic Skill: Lighted Trebuchet - Summons a trebuchet on spot that hurls projectiles on opponents, dealing +25% increased damage as ranged AoE. Uses preset basic skill Stage 1 damage multiplier. Basic skill will activate trebuchet.
Basic Skill: Default - Resets and changes to preset basic skill.
Cooldown: 1.5 seconds
Mana Cost: 30 MP
