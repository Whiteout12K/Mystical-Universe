Special Skill: Ice Pillars
Kaden creates a circle of four pillars of ice around him that shoot from the ground, dealing 250% of attack as [Water] damage.
Holding the skill will summon one pillar at a time that can be aimed towards a specific direction. The skill has four charges and all are consumed to summon remaining pillars when skill is tapped.
Summoned pillars remain active for 15 seconds and act as destructible barriers. They remain in resonance with each other, dealing 70% of attack as [Water] damage which is multiplied by the number of pillars present, up to 4.
When destroyed by any character or opponent, they explode for AoE [Water] empowered damage equal to 330% of attack and inflict [Blind] and [Freeze] on anyone who is not Kaden himself.
[Blind]
Debuff: Prevent target from attacking for 3 seconds.
[Freeze]
Debuff: Stops action for 2 seconds.
Cooldown: 16 seconds
Mana Cost: 120 MP (30 MP per pillar)
