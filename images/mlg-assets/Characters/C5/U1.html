Ultimate Skill: Transformation
River gains an aquatic state which will make her immune to non-elemental, [Fire], and [Wind] attacks, lasting the duration of the field effect. Her water attacks will absorb any incoming attack’s element, changing color, and return it as the same elemental attack in addition to her own. Multiple elements can be mixed and returned.
