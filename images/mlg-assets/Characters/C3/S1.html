Special Skill: Metal Barrier
Ted creates a large destructible metal barrier in front of him equal to his entire defense for its toughness to shield off incoming attacks in one direction. He then gives [Shield] equal to 200% of defense to self and allies.
If [Invention] is active upon using this skill, instead, give [Shield] equal to an additional 150% of defense to self and allies and the metal barrier gains twice his defense instead. Clear a stack of [Invention].
[Shield]
Buff: Creates an overshield that absorbs damage equal to a certain amount of health.
Cooldown: 16 seconds
Mana Cost: 85 MP
