Mystical Legends Game: Sneak Peak

Take a sneak peak at the upcoming Mystical Legends Game, heavily in the works! Below is the features upcoming in the game's initial release.

1.0 Release:

Play as your unique character and journey through the Mystical world, encountering friends and foes from the original book series and new canon storylines featuring some of the prominent characters. Fight opponents using a myriad of skills of your choice and become the strongest. Explore the wide world and encounter other activities to further develop your roster.

Main Story & Character Quests:

The door from the darkness as opened, revealing a luminous and captivating glow. It is at last the time for the legends to appear.

  • Chapter I - Mystical Legends: Rune of the Spellcaster
    • The beginning of one boy’s journey and another evil man’s desire for conquest. Introduction to the Mystical world and the rules of adaption.
    • Exclusive SSR character or equipment upon completion of the first chapter.
    • Prologue: Act I
    • Chapter I: Act I - Act VII
  • Chapter II - The I.T.E.R. Chronicles
    • A preview of the next major arc… technological advancement. The newly formed band of students venture towards new heights to test their newfound strength.
    • Act I - Act IV
  • Character Quests
    • Special quests and exclusive rewards related to the playable characters in the game.

World Exploration:

Explore a bountiful world consisting of the Mystical Island and its inhabitants, New York City, Pyrus Bourn’s lair, and other unique districts.

  • District 7 - Lux Urbis - A populous city at the shore of the island known for its high tech and safety, filled with bustling citizens, vehicles, and possibly crimes that only the sole academy’s finest can take down via commissions. Explore the open world city within the border such as the grand central field that separate the various famous institutes.
    • Regnum Academy - The academy grounds featuring a cafeteria, sports arena, and a special decorative dorm quad for our main character plus three allies.
    • IT Tower - A tech industry with secrets only those close to Kaden may access. Pursuit challenges by embarking up the tower.
    • Town Hall - A local public venue for gathering and buying resources.
    • Downtown - A public touristy place with all kinds of unique shops and games. Participate in the raffle and auction for exclusives.
  • District 3 - Confront the infamous Pyrus Bourn. Explore his intricate factory-style network and enter his throne room. Collect gemstones and ores through mining.
  • New York City - Where it all began… a portion of the city with tons of secrets to explore. Stride along the sidewalks, ride vehicles, and specific buildings interiors with lurking opponents.
    • OSNYC - A school established for Mystical Beings of the mainland.
    • Weeping Willows - A forest in Central Park perfect for hide and seek.
    • IT Tower - Another alternate access to the tower challenge but additional different interior rooms to explore.
    • And more…
  • Discover domains and exploration sites to gain rewards through the energy system that resets at the end of each day.
    • Revisit past story chapters to get various levels of artifacts and resources through tiered difficulties to enhance the character’s equipment and level.
    • Don’t fret for skipping dialogue, a special menu can be accessed to view past story interactions and content. Note: the physical experience may not be revisited as the world continues to change.

Hub and Leisure:

Progress enough and stay at the famous academy, Regnum, as the main spawn and gateway towards other locations. You may find unexpected things during each day-cycle.

  • Create a custom banner to showcase your influence.
  • Customize your dorm room, quad system, and have a favorite party as your dormmates.
  • Encounter visitors who may give special rewards.
  • Go through several of the school’s finest facilities to enhance your roster and visit shops.
  • Send characters to take classes to improve their XP and level to become stronger.
  • Training: Enter the arena to test your abilities and damage against a dummy with togglable strength and weaknesses, adjust its single attack intervals, and see buffs/debuffs in action. All of this is done without penalty and may be a good device to check your characters’ capabilities.

Guild Rank:

The main character and your allies forms a team known as a guild, officially approved and commissioned by the island to perform various assignments. Each level you advance will allow your characters and equipment to reach that level also through upgrading. Take on commissions to earn XP or improve city reputation.

  • Starting levels range from Level 1 (E Rank) - Level 60 (B Rank).
  • Not a cakewalk! Some ascension quests are necessary to proceed any further.
  • By improving reputation, you can gain access to a myriad of benefits, including purchasing your very own Housing which you may design the interior as you see fit with tile-slot-based precision of walls, floors, and furniture. Sleep in a bed or eat food to rejuvenate health when injured in the open world.
  • Consequently, the level cap is at Level 60, but an expansion will be expected in the near future.

Gamemodes:

Besides the regular main storyline and world, dive into various gamemodes to gather resources for improving your reputation and your arsenal of characters.

  • Arena Mode (aka, The IT Tower of Conquest):
    • Fight off waves of opponents in tiered stages.
    • Gain varying upgrades and currency rewards for completing various difficulties.
    • Progress higher an an ever expanding tower to gain better equipment and test your tactics against even tougher puzzles and opponents. No such thing as being OP forever.
  • Minigames: Partake in several minigames scattered throughout the world to earn specific prizes or upgrade materials (cost daily energy to collect reward).
    • Mystical Race: Use characters with special sprint mechanics to race, collect coins, and run through hoops within a set time to get the best record. Various stages offer better rewards. Numerous expansions for different stages include an underwater environment, aerial travel, and charging through obstacles (all these different stages require specific characters with matching attributes).
    • Food Wars: Various vendors need food and its up to you to use various characters’ elemental attacks to cook the food in different stages (heating, burning, melting, freezing… you name it). Heating isn’t just one thing, be sure to grab the correct ingredients and toss them in the right order.
    • Disk Toss: Use ranged or physical skills to attack a constantly moving frisbee and score against your opponents in an equally-numbered face off between teams.
    • Rhythmic Boom: Play various songs from the Mystical Legends series and click the right buttons at the right time of this rhythm game to earn the highest score.
  • Custom Game Creator:
    • Create games using the main stories' assets and create puzzles and challenges for anyone to take on! Have multiple saves and share world code woth others!
    • Utilize various “block” components from the world (walls, floor, decoration, mods, etc…) on three distinct layers. Player remains on the second layer and may interact with the items around them.
    • Components and decoration from Housing are marked and must be unlocked in order to be placed.
    • Generators for equipment, NPC, and enemies (with adjustable difficulty) to add continuous fun.
    • Settings that can configure the following for new tactics and playstyles: Full Roster Level or Reset Roster Level, All Skills or Limited Skills, Enable Character Skills or Disable Character Skills
  • And more to come…

Customizable Main Character:

Create your unique Mystical Being with a username, custom appearance, and upgradable skill tree. Being the featured character, you will embark on an adventures to discover new powers.

  • Starting with an Agent role, this character’s potential can extend to Healer, Defender, or Fighter, making it ideally versatile for the playstyle of your choice.
  • Choose a Mystical Species and then follow that path of unique effects and skills that aid other like-species characters. The appearance changes according to selected species.
  • The main character grows more “OP” with progression, becoming an the most invaluable flexible unit.

Characters:

Collect and play as one of the ever expansive characters. Each of them are unique and bring an expansive set of skills and playstyles to keep the progression engaging.

  • Progress characters with materials or through combat experience to enhance their XP and level. This in turn will make their base stats stronger. Add artifact sets with extra percentage stat buffs and set bonuses for further enhancement and versatility.
  • Characters come with several quick attacks, basic skills, allowing them to fight and accumulate energy gauge to unleash an ultimate skill. Expend mana or stamina to additionally use medium-strength attacks or support via the special skills. Activate conditional passives or abilities via the technical skill. Unlock additional skills for characters and mix and match skills for unlimited versatility. Witness unique artistic styles through cut animations in Special Skills and Ultimate Skills.
  • You can gain additional character dupes which are converted to star levels that gradually make the character’s passives stronger. Collect the characters (chance) and also equipment on the Standard Banner, Equipment Banner, and Skill Banner. Where's the fun when you can get a new ability for a character you least expected.
  • Play in a team of three either by swap play or single play with allies assisting actively.
  • The stronger each character’s Combat Power (CP), the better they will face against opponents of equal or less CP. Combat power is recorded additionally through the Mystical Rankings, a special badge system numerically climbing and divided in three sub-ranks. Rank through each level to enhance mana and regen, thus, overall magical power. Compare with oppoennts with the same ranking system to gauge difficulty level of any combat stage.
  • Use affinities [Light], [Dark], [Fire], [Wind], [Water], and [Earth], to counteract each other for weaknesses and strengths. Characters and opponents hit by each element gain a susceptibility against other elements also through character passives and artifacts, so beware! Most of the elements also linger on opponents as auras which can have aditional conditional mechanics. Discover some sub-affinities also!
  • Utilize the roles: Healer, Attacker, Defender, and Agent, to create the very best team formation of optimal synergy.
  • Not every challenge is combat as you’ll face even tough puzzles in which certain character passives are crucial.
  • Swap between skills and a small inventory hand to eat food to recover or buff quickly. Also hold various items and objects to place into the world for added interaction. You can always have the items you need most right on hand! Store collected resources in your inventory, but be careful, there is limited capacity and so you should deposit extra unneeded items into your storage in Housing.
  • Collect various appearances for each character to broaden your style. Look for specific character outfits in shops that cycles weekly and daily.
