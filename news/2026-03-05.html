Mystical Legends: Art Dump #1

In this post, I've decided to share some of the important art that I've done to showcase my style and the work effort to bring out the full potential of the Mystical Legends series thus far. First and foremost, I want to share what the original concepts were and how far I've come to develop an anime style colored edition that fit a general critiera.

The designs are definitely far from final but these are just the small traces of improvement and initialization. Don't mind certain details, trying to think of it too hard may ruin one's experience of the lore for the main series.
