Ultimate Skill: Metal Mode
Ted will encase metal armor around himself to gain extra defense and outward fighting power, lasting for 16 seconds.
Upon activation, Ted will gain +100% movement speed and +20% damage reduction.
Model A: Charge forward and deal [Earth] super empowered damage equal to 160% of defense to any opponent in his path. Any skills used in conjunction with this ultimate’s effect will deal +25% more damage.
Model B: If off-field, the metal armor will dissipate and form into 2 hovering robotic turret drones that will blast energy pulses towards nearby opponents, dealing 140% of defense as [Earth] damage per blast.
If two or more stacks of [Invention] are active, instead, gain +50% damage reduction, +150% movement speed, and +50% skill damage for Model A. Summon an extra drone for Model B. Clear all stacks of [Invention].
Energy Gauge: 100%
