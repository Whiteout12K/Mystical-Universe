Technical Skill: Enlightened Mode
Avery enters enlightened mode after using an ultimate skill, where he gains a special light meter which accumulates specific element points, lasting for 15 seconds.
When a [Light] element attack from an ally or self hits an opponent with an elemental aura (applied by a contributor, either self or ally), the attack will become a special discharge called [True Light Element]. That aura element will fill the light meter by 1, up to a max of 10, if the light meter is active.
[True Light Element]: A special element attack that deals additional damage based on the aura element, equal to 5% of the inflictor’s health in a small AoE.
The following are the immediate obtainable effects gained for inflictor and contributor based on the type of element aura:
> [Fire]: Damage of skills increases by +10% for 5 seconds. Gain an additional +5% damage increase for each point in the light meter.
> [Wind]:
> [Earth]: Defense increases by +2% per point on any light meter. Debuffs applied on opponents have their duration increased by +0.5 seconds per point on any light meter.
> [Water]: Health and defense increases by +25% for 5 seconds. Gain the effect [Embrace of the Goddess].
[Embrace of the Goddess]
Buff: The character will revive with 20% of max health when defeated and this effect will be removed (Cooldown: 30 seconds for each character; Duration: 5 seconds, but +1 second per point in light meter).
Additional instances of the same special attack can occur, +1 more for every 4 points in any light meter, up to max +5 instances. Simultaneous effects can be activated but require different instances with their respective auras.
The damage of the special element attack increases by +10% for each level of basic skill of inflictor.
Resonance: If Avery is not in the team, the first [Light] affinity character in the team can activate the light meter with their ultimate skill.
