Technical Skill: Goddess' Spirit Release
Ability: Avery consumes 20% of his health to summon the goddess Lynnhera, acting as an ally with all benefits, to aid him.
The goddess gains 20% of Avery’s health initially (50% max after healing) and 100% of all his other stats. She shares and consumes the same MP stat with Avery.
She uses the following skills:
Basic Skill: Spell Pulse - Lynnhera shoots a rainbow vibrating bullet that deals 125% of attack as damage and inflicts a random debuff. Can continuously charge the shot for unlimited damage scaling but must remain idle.
Special Skill: Bridge of Fortitude - Lynnhera summons a bridge, with 25% of Avery’s defense multiplied by each percentage of healing rate, that only herself and allies can stand on, elevating them and shielding them from damage until its destruction. When standing on the bridge, self and allies gain +25% mana regen.
Cooldown: 25 seconds
Special Skill: Black Hole - Lynnhera forms a black vacuum, lasting 5 seconds, that sucks opponents together, dealing continuous damage equal to 200% of attack per second.
Cooldown: 15 seconds
Lynnhera’s damage will always be an elemental advantage over her opponents (indicated in pink numbers). In normal mode, can switch between her and Avery as active controllable character.
Using this skill again while the goddess is active will sacrifice her for Avery to gain the buff [The Magni Mage]. If she is defeated in the current battle, she will grant the buff permanently to Avery and cannot be re-summoned.
The damage of Lynnhera’s skills scale up by +10% for each level of Avery’s highest level skill.
[The Magni Mage]
Buff: Gain +150% mana regen and +50% special skill cooldown for 30 seconds.
Energy Gauge: 90%
Resonance: No additional effects.
