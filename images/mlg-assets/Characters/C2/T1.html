Technical Skill: Ice Travel
Kaden will phase through the shadow realm with the creation of two ice portals. This mechanic works by synchronizing two portals, each placed separately by using this skill a total of two times. Portals will remain indefinitely while in a combat session. Use skill again after placing two portals to reset and remove them.
The two portals will allow any character to travel between them rapidly. Upon a successful warp, the character gains the buff [Shadow Miasma]. Standing within the portal after warping will maintain the effect.
If Kaden gains the effect, his special skill attacks will also do [Dark] damage during its duration.
[Shadow Miasma]
Buff: Increases [Dark] damage by +25%, increases movement speed by +100%, and +30% chance to dodge incoming attacks that would deal damage (Duration: 4 seconds).
Energy Gauge: 8% per portal
Resonance: Other [Dark] affinity characters in team who are Icelorian or Gostlynger can utilize this ability.
