Kaden will gain a [Shield] equal to 350% of his defense whenever his health reaches at or below 50% (Cooldown: 30 seconds). He will also accumulate stacks of [Flash] upon successful basic skill attack hits with a cooldown of 1 second.
He gains resonance within a party of more than 3 different affinities (dual affinity characters count both). Resonance will improve his and the team’s damage reduction by +20% and each hit from an opponent has a 50% chance to remove a debuff on on the character that is hit.
Ice Fowler: While not in combat, Kaden, when dashing, will create a trail of ice behind him as he surfs on an ice field. This state increases his movement speed by +40% and reduces stamina consumption by -50%. His movement across water is not reduced.
The secrets of District 1 are revealed when he is present. As a genius, puzzles will reveal a hint as the prodigy sees solutions effortlessly.
Alter Exclusive Rule: Ion Mustard (Alter)’s Star Levels (except base) are deactivated while as Kaden Icelander.
[Shield]
Buff: Creates an overshield that absorbs damage equal to a certain amount of health.
[Flash]
Buff: Increases attack and piercing by +1% per stack (Infinite Stacks).
The [Freeze] effect can be stacked for the duration of attacks that add it. For each stack, Kaden’s damage will increase by +5%.
[Freeze]
Debuff: Stops action for 2 seconds.
The [Flash] effect can be applied to allies also but at half the speed, along with gaining continuous stacks of [Fatigue] on self for every stack gained on self.
The Technical Skill: Ice Travel will apply effect even if Kaden stands within 10 meters of any portal.
[Fatigue]
Debuff: Decreases movement speed and max stamina by -1% per stack (Infinite Stacks).
Increases the damage of special skills by +15%.
Kaden will gain an awakened state upon reaching 30 stacks of [Fatigue] in which his eye reveals a special power, gaining the buff [Icengon].
[Icengon]
Buff: All special skill cooldown will decrease by -50%, mana regen will increase by +150%, and energy gauge accumulation increases by +25%. This effect lasts for the duration of [Fatigue] present, losing a stack every second and cannot gain stacks during the buff.
Whenever this character gains [Fatigue], gain 1% of health as mana for self and heal allies for that percentage of health. Deal an additional +5% more damage on opponents for each stack of [Freeze] applied.
The gaining of [Flash] will now be as fast as each basic skill stage, therefore increasing the rate of accumulation without a cooldown.
