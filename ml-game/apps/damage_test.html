Damage Settings

Base Damage entries are added together first. Damage Increase is then applied. Target Defense and Piercing are then used before the final crit calculation. Guaranteed Crit applies the full Crit Damage bonus. Regular Crit averages the Crit Damage bonus according to Crit Rate.

Stat Conditions / Buffs

Health, Attack, Defense, Mana, Stamina, Speed, and Piercing increases are multiplicative with the affected stat. Crit Rate, Crit Damage, Gauge Gain, Cooldown Red, and Healing Rate increases are additive. For example, +20% Crit Rate on 75% gives 95%, while +20% Attack on 2000 gives 2400.
