Basic Skill: Light Sword
Avery conjures a compressed light sword and does melee [Light] attacks.
Stage 1: Slash in several quick arcs in front, dealing 85% of attack as damage.
Stage 2: Slash in a line ahead, dealing AoE damage equal to 130% of attack. Inflict [Knockback] on hit opponents.
Stage 3: Slam into the ground, dealing AoE damage in a 1 meter radius equal to 170% of attack.
[Knockback]
Debuff: Knocks opponents back 5 meters.
