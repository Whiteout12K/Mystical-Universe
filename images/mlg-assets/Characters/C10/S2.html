Special Skill: Lynx Prowl
Kiara quickens her pace so she strikes before any refute. This skill has 9 charges which can be used anytime.
Whenever a charge is consumed, Kiara’s health will decrease by -8% and her defense will decrease by -5% for the duration of her flash state, stacking per charge.
Cooldown: 4 seconds per charge
Mana Cost: 15 MP
