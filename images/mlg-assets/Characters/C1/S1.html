Special Skill: Light Searching Spheres
Avery shoots 10 light orbs that follow and implode on contact with enemies dealing 212% of his attack as [Light] AoE ranged damage in a 1 meter radius, applying [Blind]. Projectiles will hone in on closest opponents.
Hold skill to launch projectiles in aimed direction at succession, one at a time.
If projectiles don’t find targets, they return and the active character gains 30 MP back for each sphere. If Avery is off-field, he also gets 50% of the MP as well.
[Blind]
Debuff: Prevent target from attacking for 3 seconds.
Cooldown: 14 seconds
Mana Cost: 120 MP
