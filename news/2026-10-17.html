Mystical Legends: Website and Wiki Update

In this post, I've decided to share some of the additions I've been working on for the Mystical Legends website. With the continuation of the series and the game, I wanted to have a place where you can learn more about the characters and their world alongside the books, artwork, and game development that I've shared thus far.

First and foremost, the website now has its very own Mystical Legends Wiki! There is quite a bit of information that goes into the series, and the wiki is a place to put those details together so that you can look through the characters, their powers, relationships, and the various parts of the Mystical world. The pages are still far from final, but this is another step towards bringing out the full potential of the series.



Character Pages:

I've added more information to the pages for Avery Obtainia, Lux Luces, and Kiara Katzol, with their appearance, personality, history, and abilities separated into different sections. You can also take a look at their relationships and the artwork that I've done for their various appearances.

Don't fret if you haven't read that far into the story! Certain details are hidden behind spoiler buttons, so you can choose what you want to see. There is also a Spoiler settings option in Explore to keep these hidden or reveal them as you see fit. Some of the pages do go into later events, so be careful if you want to discover those things through the books first.

Website Additions:

Besides the wiki, I've been adding a few things to make the website easier to use. The following can now be found in the Explore menu:

  • Search: Look for characters, wiki pages, skills, effects, and books without having to go through each section yourself. You can also open this with Ctrl+K or Command+K.
  • Effects: A list of the game's buffs, debuffs, and other effects. You can search for a specific one or click an effect in a character's skill description to learn what it does.
  • Spoiler settings: Choose how much of the wiki you want to reveal. Details without a confirmed book level will stay hidden unless you choose to open them yourself or show all spoilers.

The character pages also have links between the wiki and the game information, so it is easier to learn about a character and then look at their skills. For those viewing on a phone, I've made some adjustments to the skill menu so that you can keep switching between skills while reading through their descriptions.

Account Customization:

You can now go to Account after logging in to choose a profile image and background. Have the Mystical Legends torch as your profile, or choose one of the character heads instead! There is a preview of your selections before you save them, and your profile image will also appear by your username in the navigation and on your game profile.

I've also added a light and dark mode setting for the wiki that can be saved to your account. This will be used while you are logged in instead of the browser's cookie setting. If you would rather keep using the browser preference, you can select that also.

Reading the Books:

For those reading through the book series, the reader keeps your saved place and bookmarks. A Continue Reading link on the home and book pages will let you return to where you left off in that browser. You can also move between chapters or sections when the book has those navigation headings. There is no account needed for saving your reading progress.

And that's all for now, more to come in the future! There are still pages to fill in and details to improve, but I hope these additions make it easier to explore what I've been working on. With each part added, I feel the vision of the series coming alive more and more. Thanks for reading, and stay tuned for more updates!
