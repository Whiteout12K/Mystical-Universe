Ultimate Skill: Limitless Ice Beam
Kaden charges up a stronger ice beam, gaining high range, that he blasts in two directions from his hands, dealing continuous [Water] damage per half-second equal to 240% of attack for 7 seconds.
Hit opponents will gain the [Freeze] debuff and any subsequent hits during this state will increase their damage taken by +100%. The hit opponents will also gain debuff immunity reduction of -30% for the duration of skill.
