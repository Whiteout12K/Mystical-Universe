Special Skill: Mythical Healing
Avery raises his hand and creates a circle of magic that lasts for 12 seconds and heals all allies for 3% of his max health per second. Applies a [Shield] to all allies equal to 10% of max health a single time.
Deals 125% of attack as [Light] damage to all opponents within the circle per second. Reduce their movement speed by -25%. Each successful hit will fill self and allies’ energy gauge by +3%.
[Shield]
Buff: Creates an overshield that absorbs damage equal to a certain amount of health.
Cooldown: 16 seconds
Mana Cost: 210 MP
