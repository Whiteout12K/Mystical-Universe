Technical Skill: Transformation
Ability: Avery lets magic pour out and changes parts of his body into water, gaining an aquatic state. By doing so, his skills will become [Water] elemental attacks until this technical skill is used again.
While in this state, he gains [Shield] +50% more effectively and consumes a single percentage per second, increasing his and allies’ damage reduction by +35% until depletion. This functions even while off-field.
Speed is enhanced by +25%. Flight Mode will turn Avery into a stream of water instead called Aqua Mode; collision with opponents in this mode will deal 0.5% of health multiplied by each percentage of healing rate as [Water] damage every 0.3 seconds.
Resonance: Other characters that are Water Spirit or Naiad species can utilize this ability, including Aqua Mode. If the character has an element affinity other than [Water], then their element is not overridden by this skill.
