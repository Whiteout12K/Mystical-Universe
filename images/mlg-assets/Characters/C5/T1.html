Technical Skill: A Fox's Tale
River’s Special Skill: Water Ball can absorb incoming elemental attacks of opponents (this does not get rid of the incoming attack), converting to a different color.
The absorbed elements will increase that element’s damage for allies and self by +20% for 10 seconds. Opponents hit by the water ball will have their resistance to absorbed elements be reduced by -20% for 10 seconds.
[Water] damage will increase automatically as that is River’s innate magical affinity.
These effects additionally increase by +1% per stack of [Overwhelming Mana].
Gain a stack of [Overwhelming Mana] when mixing at least two other elements at once (Cooldown: 3 seconds).
