Technical Skill: Ultimate Arsenal
Ability: Upon using this skill, various effects will activate upon having varying stacks of [Invention]:
1 stack, gain the effect [Gamer]. 2 stacks, gain the effect [Tinkering]. 3+ stacks, turn into Special Skill: Hoverboard which may be used anytime. Stacks do not get consumed when counting and using this skill.
[Gamer]
Buff: Increases defense by +30% for 15 seconds.
[Tinkering]
Buff: Crit rate increases by +25% for 15 seconds.
Special Skill: Hoverboard - Ted jumps onto a hoverboard, hovers in the air for a moment with short invulnerability, and crashes his way through his opponents, dealing 325% of attack and 225% of defense as [Fire] and [Earth] AoE damage. The damage scales by +10% for each level of the equipped basic skill.
Resonance: No additional effects. Exclusive to Ted.
