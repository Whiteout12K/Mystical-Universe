Special Skill: Arsenal of Ice
Kaden will create various objects of ice to aid in his battle. Hold down skill to rotate between a list of items; then letting go will select the specified item.
Special Skill: Ice Beam - A directable beam, with a medium range, that does continuous damage equal to 175% of attack as [Water] damage per half-second for 4 seconds.
Special Skill: Ice Mass - Kaden hurls a single boulder of ice that hits an opponent, dealing 600% of attack as [Water] empowered damage.
Special Skill: Javelins’ Dance - Kaden summons a ring of 10 (scales by level, rounded up) javelins around him that he hurls to opponents, dealing AoE [Water] damage equal to 160% of attack per javelin. When he is off-field, the javelins will be thrusted in 1 second intervals until no javelins are left.
Cooldown: 12 seconds
Mana Cost: 120 MP
