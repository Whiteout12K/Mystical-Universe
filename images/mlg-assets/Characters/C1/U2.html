Ultimate Skill: Mystical Legends, Unite!
When the dire time comes, calls upon the aid of all Mystical Legends allies to arrive at the forefront of the battle for a prolonged duration.
Each ally retains their stats and equipment if owned, otherwise won’t be called. They start with 40% of their base stats. When summoned, their special skills are on their respective cooldowns instead of immediately being usable.
Besides Avery himself, each Mystical Legends ally that joins will lock the use of a team member (in order of their party placement), but their Star Level passives and Technical Skill passives still function. At max 3 will be locked. There is no limit to the number of summoned allies that join.
Mystical Legends:
> Avery Obtainia (self)
> Kaden Icelander
> Teddric Tech
> River Grant
> Kiara Katzol
> Lucy Terner
> Amos Black
Skill can be used only once per battle. While out of combat, the skill can be used for free and the summoned allies will leave after a short duration.
As long as this skill is equipped, the energy gauge for the team increases by half its usual speed.
Energy Gauge: 100% of the team’s energy gauge.
