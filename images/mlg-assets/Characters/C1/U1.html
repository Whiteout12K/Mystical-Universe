Ultimate Skill: Limitless Light Beam
Avery’s body begins to glow with immense light. He shoots a beam of laser that can be directed towards opponents for a short duration dealing 400% of attack as [Light] ranged damage per second. The rate increases to per half-second when hitting a single target.
Hit opponents receive the [Blind] effect. Upon activation, allies and self receive the buff [Enlightened].
[Blind]
Debuff: Prevent target from attacking for 3 seconds.
[Enlightened]
Buff: Increases attack by +15% and receives +15% damage reduction for 20 seconds.
Energy Gauge: 100%
